# Customer Enablement — Lesson 2 Exercise

## Purpose

Apply the Customer Enablement machinery from Lesson 2 to a hypothetical customer journey.

The exercise is designed to test how naturally an enablement-oriented approach emerges without requiring prior familiarity with formal enablement terminology or specific enablement tools.

Everything about TERP below is **hypothetical**, inspired by the Tipuli job description rather than presented as fact about the product.

## Scenario: New Therapy Clinic Onboarding to TERP

### Customer

**Shaked Therapy Center**, a multidisciplinary clinic with:

- 12 therapists
- 2 administrative staff
- approximately 180 active patients

### Current State

The clinic currently uses a mixture of:

- spreadsheets
- Google Calendar
- WhatsApp
- Word documents
- a basic billing application

Management has decided to move the clinic's operations onto TERP.

### Users

**Clinic manager — Yael**  
Owns the transition and needs visibility into clinic operations.

**Administrator — Michal**  
Handles scheduling, patient intake, and billing administration.

**Therapists**  
Need to see their schedules and maintain whatever treatment/patient records TERP requires.

**New employees**  
Eventually need to be brought into the system without Tipuli effectively onboarding each one.

### Assumed TERP Workflows

For purposes of the exercise, assume TERP supports:

- organization and account configuration
- adding staff and assigning roles/permissions
- patient creation/import
- scheduling
- treatment documentation
- billing
- management reporting

These are exercise assumptions, not researched claims about TERP functionality.

## Business Objective

Tipuli does not merely want Shaked to *have TERP*.

Six weeks after implementation, the clinic should be conducting its normal day-to-day operations through TERP with minimal dependence on Tipuli personnel.

A provisional definition of successful enablement is therefore:

> Shaked's staff can independently perform the TERP workflows required for their respective jobs, and the clinic has incorporated those workflows into normal operations.

## Available Resources

Assume the Customer Enablement Specialist has access to:

- Product
- Customer Success
- Support
- documentation authoring
- video creation
- webinars/live training
- in-product guidance

Engineering can make product changes, but those changes compete for development resources.

Assume some product documentation already exists, but there is **no coherent enablement program**.

## Assignment

Do not design all of the content yet. Design the **customer journey**.

Reason through these five questions:

### 1. What does Shaked need to accomplish, and in what order?

Do not start from TERP's feature list. Start from the clinic's transition from its existing processes to operating successfully in TERP.

### 2. Who needs to learn what?

Yael, Michal, and the therapists probably do not need identical onboarding.

### 3. Where should human intervention be used, and where should it deliberately be avoided?

Apply the maturity-model principle from Lesson 1: identify activities that might initially require a Customer Success Manager or trainer but should eventually become standardized, scalable, or self-service.

### 4. Where are the likely failure points?

The actual failure points are unknown, so hypotheses are acceptable if clearly identified as such.

More importantly: determine how the actual failure points would be discovered.

### 5. What would demonstrate that Shaked is successfully enabled?

Do not rely only on content-consumption measures such as "watched all four videos."

Identify observable customer behavior that would demonstrate successful enablement.

## Constraint

Do **not** design the answer around Tipuli's job posting.

Do not try to demonstrate knowledge of webinars, certification tracks, Pendo, help centers, or other particular enablement mechanisms simply because they appeared in the posting.

Those are possible tools, not requirements for the solution.

Approach the problem as though someone said:

> We just sold this clinic TERP. Six weeks from now they need to be running on it without depending on us. Figure out how we get them there.

The answer should emphasize reasoning over polish.

Before answering the five questions, Yechiel proposed first creating a **workflow matrix organized by role**, to establish who needs what before designing enablement interventions.

That answer and the remainder of the exercise will be preserved separately once completed.
